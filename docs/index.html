What Tetra does for you

Files to and from your PC in a second

Share anything to Send to computer on the phone, or Send to phone on the PC. It goes over your own Wi-Fi: no cable, no cloud. If the computer is away, it goes when it is back.

Call from your computer

Click a number in Contacts on the PC and your phone dials it. A call bubble on the screen answers, holds, mutes and hangs up.

Your data plan, watched

Tell Tetra your plan once. It notifies you at 50 %, 80 % and 100 %, or at any steps you choose, and forecasts the end of the month before you get there.

Calendar and timers, with AI

Built-in skills let Claude, ChatGPT or any assistant that can use your computer import a timetable, plan your week or start a timer. They simply edit the calendar's and timers' files.

Photos that know who and where

Tags, live photos, and People and Documents collections from face recognition that runs on the device. Find photos by place*, and bring your Google Photos over (Takeout import on the computer).

Scan any page clean

Filters and automatic cropping find and straighten the page: all the tools you need to make a perfect digital copy, for as many pages as you like.

Hide what should stay private

Redact PDFs and photos so the hidden part is removed from the file itself, not just covered. Tetra finds the eyes in a photo and covers them with black bars or stars, and finds text to hide.

Office and PDF, good enough

Word, Excel and PowerPoint open and save as themselves, compatible enough for everyday work. PDF editing and page organising. And a phone layout you can actually use.

Maps that work offline*

Search, places, routes by car, on foot and by bike, voice guidance and Android Auto, all on the device. *Maps and finding photos by place cover Greece only for now.

See it work

Every applet, on every screen

One Home, one folder, the same applets on Android and Linux. Also inside: Scanner, Codes and a Text editor.

Every applet in one place, with its shortcuts; edit mode sets their size and order.

Timeline, collections, people and documents found on the device, a map, search and an editor.

Tags, favourites, folder colours, a Trash, an icon for every kind of file, and files sent both ways.

Notes and checklists with colours, labels, pins and history.

Words, Tables, Slides and PDF, with the ribbon, rulers, charts and themes.

Day, week, month and agenda, repeats, reminders, birthdays from Contacts.

Timers that ring on whichever device you are at.

Search, saved places, lists and trips, routes and turn-by-turn guidance. *Greece only for now.

vCard files with faces from Photos, favourites, labels and each person's calls.

The dialer: Recents, Favourites, blocking and spam lookup; on the computer, the call bubble.

Usage per app on mobile and Wi-Fi, the hotspot, Wi-Fi signal and drops.

Fast, on purpose

Every applet was measured on a phone and reworked until it opens in a blink: Contacts right after start went from 3.3 s to 0.19 s. On the computer, Photos opens again in 71 ms instead of 741.
