A dozen apps, one place, your devices

Each applet opens from Home and shares one folder. The same applets run on Android phones and tablets and on Linux. Faces, labels, text recognition, maps and routes are worked out on the device: no account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Tetra is free software and will stay free. Also inside: Scanner, Codes and a Text editor.

Every applet in one place, each with its shortcuts. Edit mode sets their size and order.

Timeline, collections, people, documents found for you, a map, search and an editor. Faces and labels are worked out on the device.

Your Tetra folder with tags, favourites, folder colours, Trash and an icon for every kind of file.

Notes and checklists with colours, labels, pins and history, and keyboard shortcuts.

Words, Tables, Slides and PDF: Microsoft and LibreOffice files open and save as themselves, with the ribbon, rulers, charts and themes.

Plain iCalendar files: day, week, month and agenda, repeats, reminders, birthdays from Contacts.

Timers that ring on whichever device you are at, pinned on Home.

OpenStreetMap on the device: search, places, lists and trips, car, walk and bike routes, voice guidance and Android Auto. *Greece only for now.

Your contacts as vCard files, with faces from Photos, favourites, labels and the calls with each person.

A dialer with Recents, blocking and spam lookup. On the computer, the phone dials for you and a call bubble answers.

What each app uses on mobile data and Wi-Fi, plan alerts and forecast, the hotspot, Wi-Fi signal and drops.

Screenshots use invented sample data. Tap one to see it full size.

Fast, on purpose

Every applet was measured and reworked: nothing is read while a screen draws, small indexes in the cache, read-ahead after Home's first frame, lists made for thousands of items.
