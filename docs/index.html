A dozen apps, one place, your devices

Each applet opens from Home and shares one folder. The same applets run on Android phones and tablets and on Linux. Faces, labels, text recognition, maps and routes are worked out on the device: no account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Tetra is free software and will stay free.

Fast, on purpose

Every applet was measured and reworked: nothing is read while a screen draws, small indexes in the cache, read-ahead after Home's first frame, lists made for thousands of items.
